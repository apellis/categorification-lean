# Standard modules and standard filtrations

## Source

Webster, arXiv:1309.3796v2, §5.1 (preorder, Definition 5.1) and §5.4 (Proposition 5.20, Corollary 5.21):

- Preorder (before Definition 5.1): `(i, κ) ≤ (i', κ')` when for every red strand `j`, the sum
  of the simple roots of the black strands left of red strand `j` in `(i, κ)` is at most the
  same sum for `(i', κ')`, in the order of the positive root cone. Moving black strands left
  (or red strands right) goes up.
- Definition 5.1: `U^κ_i ⊆ P̃^κ_i` is generated by the images of all maps
  `P̃^{κ'}_{i'} → P̃^κ_i` with `(i', κ') ≥ (i, κ)`; the standard module is
  `S^κ_i = P̃^κ_i / U^κ_i`. When `κ(1) = 0` the action factors through `T^λ`.
- Corollary 5.21: `T^λ` is standardly stratified for this preorder, with standard modules the
  standard quotients of the indecomposable projectives. Its proof uses Proposition 5.20: each
  projective has a filtration by standard modules, ordered by the preorder.

Reading of Definition 5.1: with `≥` non-strict, the identity map of `P̃^κ_i` would make
`U = P̃` and `S = 0`. The definition of `S_α` below it and the proof of Corollary 5.22 use the
strict order; the card reads `≥` as `>`.

## Lean

- `Stendhal.blackCount s r i` (`StandardModule.lean`): the number of black `i`-strands of the
  loading `s` left of red strand `r`.
- `StendhalLE s t := ∀ r i, blackCount s r i ≤ blackCount t r i`; `StendhalLT` its strict part.
- `seqIdem Q Λ s hs` (`LeftStandardModule.lean`): the image of `e(s)` in `T^λ`.
- `leftUpper Q Λ s hs`: the left `T^λ`-submodule spanned by `e(t) a e(s)` with
  `StendhalLT s t`.
- `leftStandard Q Λ s hs`: `T^λ e(s)` modulo `leftUpper`.
- `stdModule Q Λ ν c` (`ProjectiveStandardFiltration.lean`): `leftStandard` as an object of
  `ModuleCat (TensorProductAlgebra …)`.
- `ModuleFiltration.HasFiltration L m M` (`Categorification/Algebra/ModuleFiltration.lean`):
  `M` has a finite filtration whose successive subquotients, from the bottom, are isomorphic to
  `L c` for `c` in the list `m`; the zero module has the empty filtration.

## Conventions

- Left modules throughout (Webster: right modules). The horizontal reflection exchanges them.
- The comparison of root sums is coordinatewise in the multiplicities of the simple roots,
  which is the order on the positive cone `ℕ[I]`.
- Webster defines `U` inside `P̃ = e T̃^λ` and then passes to `T^λ` when `κ(1) = 0`; the library
  works in `T^λ` directly.
- Indexing: standard modules are indexed by loadings, not by isomorphism classes of
  indecomposable projectives; equivalent loadings (`StendhalLE` both ways) are not merged.

- `scripts/Faithfulness/WebsterSanity.lean`: `stendhalLE_refl` (the preorder is reflexive) and
  `not_stendhalLT_self` (the strict part is irreflexive, so `U_s` does not contain the
