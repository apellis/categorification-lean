# The tensor product algebra `T^λ`

## Source

Webster, arXiv:1309.3796v2, §4.1–4.2:

- Definition 4.4 (degree): a black/black crossing of colours `i, j` has degree `−⟨αᵢ, αⱼ⟩`; a
  dot on a black `i`-strand has degree `⟨αᵢ, αᵢ⟩ = 2dᵢ`; a black `i`/red `λ` crossing has degree
  `⟨αᵢ, λ⟩ = dᵢλⁱ`.
- Definition 4.5: `T̃` is the algebra of Stendhal diagrams modulo the KLR relations (2.5a–2.5g)
  among black strands, together with: black dots and crossings pass through red strands, with a
  correction term in the triple-point relation (4.1a) when the two black strands have the same
  colour; and a black `i`-strand crossing a red `λ`-strand twice equals the straight strands
  with `λⁱ` dots on the black strand, on either side (4.2).
- Definition 4.6: `T` is the quotient of `T̃` by the two-sided ideal generated by violated
  diagrams (diagrams with a black strand at the far left of some horizontal slice;
  Definition 4.3).
- Definition 4.7: `T^λ` is the part of `T` where the red strands are labelled
  `λ₁, …, λ_ℓ` from left to right.

## Lean

- `Stendhal.stendhalQ Q Λ` (`Categorification/WeightedKLR/RedStrands/Stendhal.lean`): the KLR
  polynomials on the colour set `I ⊕ Fin ℓ` (black colours and red labels): `Q i j` between
  black strands; `u^{λ_r^i}` for black `i` left of red `r`; `v^{λ_r^i}` for red `r` left of
  black `i`; `0` between reds.
- `Stendhal.Rel`: the KLR relations for `stendhalQ`, dots on red strands are zero, and
  crossings of two red strands are zero.
- `StendhalAlgebraAllOrders k Q Λ ν = RingQuot (Rel …)`: diagrams with all red orders.
- `StendhalAlgebra`: the corner at the sum of red-ordered idempotents (`IsRedOrdered`: red
  labels increase from left to right). This is `T̃^λ`.
- `StendhalAlgebra.violatedIdeal`: the two-sided ideal generated by the red-ordered idempotents
  `e(i)` whose leftmost strand is black (`IsViolated`).
- `TensorProductAlgebra k Q Λ ν`: the quotient of `T̃^λ` by `violatedIdeal`.
- `tensorProductGrade` (`StendhalGrading.lean`), from `stendhalGradingDatum`: dot on red `2`
  (irrelevant, red dots vanish), black/red crossing `dᵢ λ_r^i`, black/black from the KLR grading.

## Conventions

- Strand positions are read left to right; the first polynomial variable `u` is the dot on the
  left strand of a crossing pair.
- Reviewers should check the sign and form of the correction term in (4.1a) against the KLR
  triple-point relation for `stendhalQ` (the generic relation with `Q_{ir}(y₁, y₂) = y₁^{λⁱ}`).
- The library defines `T^λ` with any `Q`; the KLR relations among black strands use the
  library's KLR conventions (`Categorification.KLR`).
